Business Background

    Nature Defend Essentials is a proposed business focused on manufacturing and selling Deo-Spray made with natural and carefully selected ingredients. The product is developed as an environmentally responsible alternative to traditional deodorants.

Business Story

    The business was developed in response to the growing interest in natural and sustainable personal-care products. It aims to provide consumers with a deodorant that supports everyday freshness while promoting health and environmental responsibility.

Mission

    To provide a safe, natural, and sustainable deodorant that meets customer needs while promoting health, environmental responsibility, and customer satisfaction.

Vision

To become an eco-friendly and health-conscious deodorant brand trusted by customers for providing natural and sustainable personal-care products.

Business Goals

1.Provide a Deo-Spray that meets customer needs and expectations
2.Build customer trust and loyalty
3.Achieve at least 40% repeat purchases from satisfied users
4.Establish brand awareness through digital marketing
5.Expand market presence
6.Promote eco-friendly and health-conscious personal care

Product Description

Deo-Spray is a natural and environmentally responsible alternative to traditional deodorants. It provides everyday freshness and helps reduce body odor while using carefully selected ingredients.

Ingridients

1. Purified Water
2. Organic Witch Hazel Hydrosol
3. Potassium Alum Sulfate (Tawas)
4. Lemon Essential Oil
5. Polysorbate / Solubilizer
6.Cosmetic-Grade Phenoxyethanol

Product Features

1. Natural formulation
2. Fresh lemon scent
3. Helps manage body odor
4. Convenient spray application
5. Eco-friendly positioning
6. Suitable for health-conscious consumers
7. Designed as an alternative to conventional deodorants

Target Users

1. Teenagers and adults aged 13 and above
2. Students
3. Office workers
4. People with sensitive skin
5. Eco-friendly consumers Share

Organizational Structure

Our team is composed of dedicated partners, each responsible for a specific area of the business.

1. Partner in Charge of Overall Management – Leads the overall business operations and ensures that goals and plans are properly carried out.
2. Partner in Charge of Production and Quality Control – Oversees the production process, ensures quality standards are met, and maintains efficient production.
3. Partner in Charge of Finance and Administration – Handles budgeting, financial planning, office administration, and daily operational support.
4. Partner in Charge of Production and Sales – Focuses on product sales, promotional activities, and supporting the production process.
5. Partner in Charge of Production and Inventory – Monitors supplies and inventory, maintains records, and ensures product quality and availability.
